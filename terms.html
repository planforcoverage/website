The SMS program

Plan For Coverage sends text messages about the quote or coverage request you submit through this website. These messages may include follow-up questions, quote information and replies to your request.

What opting in means

Checking the text message box on a form means you agree to receive text messages from Plan For Coverage about your request at the number you provided. The box is optional, and you can submit the form without checking it.

Message frequency and cost

Message frequency may vary. Message and data rates may apply, according to your mobile plan.

How to opt out or get help

Reply STOP to any message to opt out. Reply HELP for help, call (424) 362-9253 or email ebecker@planforcoverage.com.

Consent is not required

Consent to receive text messages is not a condition of purchase or service.

Privacy

I do not sell your mobile number, and I do not share it with lead buyers, marketers or other agents. See the privacy policy for how your information is handled.

Carrier liability

Mobile carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.
