Major Medical

Comprehensive health insurance — the plan that covers doctor visits, hospital care, prescriptions and preventive services. Available through the ACA marketplace (Covered California and other state exchanges) or directly from carriers.

Who it's for

  • Individuals and families without employer coverage
  • Self‑employed people and small‑business owners
  • Early retirees not yet eligible for Medicare
  • Anyone who lost coverage through a job change, move or divorce

What it typically covers

  • Preventive care at no cost
  • Office visits, specialists and urgent care
  • Hospitalization and surgery
  • Prescription drugs
  • Maternity, mental health and the other essential health benefits

Good to know

Open enrollment runs each fall for coverage starting January 1. Outside that window you need a qualifying life event — losing coverage, moving, marriage, a new child — to enroll.

Subsidies based on household income can lower the premium substantially. I'll run the numbers with you before we look at plans.

Networks matter. The cheapest plan is rarely the right one if your doctor isn't in it. Bring a list of the doctors and prescriptions you want to keep.

Not sure which plan fits? Let's talk it through.

A 15‑minute call is usually enough to narrow it down. No cost, no obligation, and your information stays with me.
