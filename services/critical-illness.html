Critical Illness

A lump‑sum payment when you're diagnosed with a covered condition — commonly cancer, heart attack, stroke, kidney failure or major organ transplant. Use it for treatment costs, travel, time off work or keeping the mortgage paid.

Who it's for

  • Anyone with a family history of cancer or heart disease
  • Sole earners and self‑employed people whose income stops when they can't work
  • People who want a defined safety net beyond their health plan

What it typically covers

  • Cancer (invasive and, often, a partial benefit for early‑stage)
  • Heart attack and stroke
  • Kidney failure and organ transplant
  • Optional riders for additional conditions and wellness screenings

Good to know

Benefit amounts typically range from $10,000 to $50,000 or more; premiums depend on age, tobacco use and the amount chosen.

Many plans include a small annual wellness benefit that pays you for routine screenings, which can offset much of the premium.
