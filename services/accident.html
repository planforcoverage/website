Accident Insurance

Pays a fixed cash benefit when you're hurt — a broken bone, a sprain, stitches, an ER visit. The money goes to you, not the hospital, so you can put it toward your deductible, a missed paycheck or anything else.

Who it's for

  • Families with active kids
  • People on high‑deductible health plans
  • Anyone who works with their hands or plays hard on weekends

What it typically covers

  • Emergency room and urgent care visits
  • Fractures, dislocations and lacerations
  • Ambulance transport
  • Follow‑up care and physical therapy
  • Accidental death and dismemberment

Good to know

Accident plans are usually inexpensive and have no network: you see whoever you want and file a claim afterward.

They don't replace major medical. They sit beside it to cover the out‑of‑pocket costs that pile up fast after an injury.
