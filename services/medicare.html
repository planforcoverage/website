Medicare referrals

I'm not appointed to sell Medicare Advantage, Medicare Supplement or Part D plans, and I don't pretend otherwise. If you're turning 65, retiring, or already on Medicare and want a review, I'll connect you with a Medicare specialist I trust.

When a referral makes sense

  • You're within a few months of your 65th birthday
  • You're leaving employer coverage after 65
  • You want someone to review your current Medicare plan during Annual Enrollment (October 15 – December 7)
  • A spouse is on Medicare while you still need an individual plan — I can handle your side and coordinate the rest

What I can still help with

Dental, vision, hospital indemnity and critical illness plans are available to people on Medicare and often fill gaps that Medicare leaves. Those I can quote directly.

For official information, visit medicare.gov or call 1‑800‑MEDICARE.
