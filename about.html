About

Plan For Coverage is a one‑person brokerage run by Eric Becker. When you call, you get me — not a queue.

Why a solo broker

Most people buy health insurance once a year, under time pressure, from a website that doesn't explain much. The big agencies fix that with volume: a form, a lead, a dialer. I'd rather fix it with attention. I take on a manageable number of clients, learn their situations, and stay available year‑round when a claim gets denied or a doctor leaves the network.

Licensing

I hold Life and Health licenses and am based in California, with non‑resident licenses in Arizona, Florida, Georgia, Michigan, Ohio, Oregon, Tennessee, Texas and Utah. California Insurance License #0I16359, National Producer Number 16853098.

I'm a Covered California certified insurance agent, so I can enroll you on the exchange with any subsidy you qualify for, as well as off‑exchange and in the other states I serve.

I am not appointed to sell Medicare products. If you need Medicare help I'll refer you to a specialist rather than guess.

How I'm paid

Insurance carriers pay brokers a commission that's already built into the premium. You pay the same price whether you enroll through me, through the exchange directly, or through a call center — so there's no fee for my help.
