Privacy policy

Last updated October 2026

What I collect

When you request a quote I collect the information you provide: name, contact details, state of residence, household size and any notes about your coverage needs. If you enroll in a plan, carriers will require additional information such as dates of birth, income (for subsidy eligibility) and Social Security numbers, which are collected on the carrier's or exchange's secure forms, not on this website.

How I use it

Your information is used to prepare quotes, enroll you in coverage you choose, service your policy and contact you about your request. I do not sell your information, and I do not share it with lead generators, marketers or other agents.

Text messages

By providing a phone number and checking the consent box, you agree to receive calls and text messages from Plan For Coverage about your request. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out or HELP for help. Consent is not a condition of purchase.

Third parties

The quote form is processed by a form service that transmits submissions to me by email. This site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log standard server data such as IP addresses. No analytics or advertising cookies are set by this site.

Your choices

You may ask me to correct or delete the information I hold about you at any time by emailing ebecker@planforcoverage.com, subject to record‑keeping requirements imposed by insurance regulators.
